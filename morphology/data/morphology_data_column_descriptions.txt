Column name	Description
ID	Sample ID that links genetic data with macrophotos and 3D annotations
Species	Visual species identity in the field (often incorrect)
Species_mpha	Species identity based on initial genetic assessment; if available. MPHA has been divided up into 3 lineages
Depth	Plot depth
Date	This is the date of the dive. Format is Month/Day/Year
Site	Name of the dive site
Original_macrophoto_file_name	The original file name; as named by the Olympus TG5 camera
Diver	Name of the diver who is wearing the Paralenz and did the tissue sampling
Dive_Num	Dive number of the day. Obtained from the directory where the video footage was saved.
Septa.#	Septa counts were attempted for 15 corallites. # refers to corallite number. Corallites 1-15 are identified in annotated macrophotographs that exist in two places: Nelly's laptop and an external hard drive called 'Nelly's Madracis Backup' that is either at CAS or with Jennifer Hoey (jahoey13@gmail.com). Data taken by Nelly Batista (nellybatista9th@gmail.com). 
IC.Distance.#	"Intercorallite distances were attempted for five groups of four corallites per photograph. # refers to intercorallite distance number. Distance was measured between a central corallite and three adjacent ones. The intercorallite distances are identified in annotated macrophotographs that exist in two places: Nelly's laptop and an external hard drive called 'Nelly's Madracis Backup' that is either at CAS or with Jennifer Hoey (jahoey13@gmail.com). Data taken by Nelly Batista (nellybatista9th@gmail.com) using ImageJ. 

Known scales were either the white squares in the corners of the photographs or a yellower ruler that was placed into the photograph if the camera could not be placed immediately adjacent to the colony (i.e.; colony is in a crevice). Initally; it was thought that the white squares were 1 mm on each side; but this was incorrect. The white squares are 2mm on each side; so that is why Nelly's data needed to be 'rescaled'. Nelly's measurements were rescaled by Jennifer using R to multiply Nelly's measurements by 2."
Notes	Notes from Nelly about the photo quality; corallites; or scale.
Yellow.Ruler	A 'yes' indicates that the yellow ruler was used to set the scale in ImageJ. 'Yellow ruler' measurements tended to be larger than 'white square' ones.
Angled_Ruler	Qualitative description of if the yellow ruler was angled or not; which could further influence the measured distance.
Blurry_Square	Qualitative description of how in focus the photograph was: Clear; Medium; Blurry or NA
Yellow_Ruler_Backgroud	Qualitative description of if the yellow ruler appeared to be in the same plane as the coral; or if additional depth was introduced; which could influence the measured distance.
Area.#	Corallite area was attempted for 15 corallites. # refers to corallite number. Corallites 1-15 are identified in annotated macrophotographs that exist in two places: Giorgio's laptop and a Google Drive folder: https://drive.google.com/drive/folders/1h16sg20Pfoj_g6Efi69Y-EUCdR_MU9bB. Most measurements done by Giorgio Terzi (giorgioterzi0028@gmail.com) and then completed by Nelly Batista. Partway through measurements; Giorgio discovered a better way to save the traced corallites using the ROI manager in ImageJ; which allows measurements to quickly be redone if the scale changes. The methods are described here: https://docs.google.com/document/d/1Zcwz7BIrWdxAO1ob20_IYMwcVF0aNojX/edit#heading=h.zh0j68bgx56e. This became relevant when it was discovered that the white square scales had 2mm sides (rather than 1mm). Corallite area couldn't be meaured for many corals because a scale was not in the photograph.
Rescaled_Nelly	A 'yes' indicates that Nelly remeasured corallite area for this photo because the incorrect 1mm scale was initially used
Notes.y	Notes about the scale that was used: 1mm; 2mm or ruler
More.Notes	Notes about the photo; corallites or saved output
